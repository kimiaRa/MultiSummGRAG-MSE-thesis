Descriptive replication/stability analysis, NOT a significance test. 3 new Full and 3 new B1 Phase-5 prose generations per city, generated by `evaluation/run_b1_replication.py`, analysed by `evaluation/analyze_b1_replication.py`. The original one-shot Full/B1 reports were NOT included in this replicate set.

## WITHIN-CONDITION STABILITY

- **Full-vs-Full** (n=15 pairs pooled across cities): mean 87.28%, median 87.1%, range 68.9-100.0%.
- **B1-vs-B1** (n=15 pairs pooled across cities): mean 85.91%, median 82.2%, range 72.7-100.0%.

## FULL-VS-B1 STABILITY

- **Full-vs-B1** (n=45 pairs pooled across cities): mean 68.45%, median 68.2%, range 60.7-76.0%.

**Descriptive contrast** (mean Full-vs-B1 overlap minus pooled within-condition overlap, where pooled-within combines every Full-Full and B1-B1 pairwise value into one list before averaging): **-18.14 percentage points**.

A substantially negative value (between-condition overlap much lower than within-condition overlap) would support a systematic GraphRAG-context effect distinguishable from ordinary generation variability. A value close to zero, or overlapping the within-condition range, means the original one-shot Full/B1 difference is not distinguishable from ordinary Phase-5 run-to-run variability on this evidence alone. No arbitrary significance threshold is applied to this number -- read it against the ranges above, not against a cutoff.

## INTERPRETATION LIMITS

- n=3 per condition per city is a **stability/replication analysis**, not a powered significance test.
- **No p-values or causal-effect estimates are justified by this data** and none are computed anywhere in this script.
- A lower Full-vs-B1 overlap than Full-vs-Full/B1-vs-B1 overlap (see the descriptive contrast above) would be evidence **consistent with** a systematic GraphRAG-context effect -- it does not, on its own, prove one.
- Overlap of the three distributions would mean ordinary stochastic Phase-5 variation remains a plausible explanation for at least some of the differences observed in the original one-shot Full/B1 comparison.
