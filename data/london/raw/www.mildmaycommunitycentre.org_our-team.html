Hi, I’m Kirsty,

Community Arts Coordinator

Who am I?

I’m a puppet-maker, visual artist and socially engaged practitioner, devising creative workshops and theatrical productions.

I live on a little narrow boat cruising around the canals and rivers of London.

I enjoy spending time maintaining my boat and being an activist with the National Bargee Travellers Association

My Interests

I spend my spare time visiting exhibitions and watching live performances and theatre, especially puppetry.

My two biggest achievements

In 2011 I was selected to play for Team Scotland in the first Roller Derby World Cup in Toronto, Canada. I was captain for the roller derby team the Lethal Weapons and regularly bouted across the UK and Europe.

In 2021 I was commissioned by Emergency Exit Arts to create my first live theatre show which was a collaborative project with Jess Sanders, resulting in an Immersive Augmented Reality experience “The Glitch in the Wardrobe’.

One quirky fact about me

After my roller derby career ended, I briefly trained as a pro wrestler, which is somewhat amusing as I am a mere 5’1 inches.
