Make a Difference While Celebrating

Companies, organisations, and private individuals use our catering services for weddings, birthday parties, Christmas parties, and more, using all surplus food and supporting our charity.

Event Enquiry

Catering with a cause Why Choose Us?

By choosing Surplus to Supper to cater for your event, you're not only selecting a unique, tasty, and sustainable catering option—you're also supporting a volunteer-led charity powered by over 200 passionate individuals.
Whether it’s a finger buffet, a wedding breakfast or a five-course -black tie event our experienced team are ready and waiting to discuss your requirements.
And remember, for each meal you buy you help us to provide another to those in need (Buy One Feed One Free). Your event becomes part of something bigger: supporting a grassroots movement and enabling Surplus to Supper  to make a real difference locally to those who need it most - one meal at a time.

Food that does good Buy One, Feed One Free

For every plate we serve, we donate a free meal to a local charity, ensuring that the impact of your event extends beyond your celebration. This initiative helps us tackle food insecurity and contributes to our goal of reducing waste, all while making your event both memorable and meaningful.
