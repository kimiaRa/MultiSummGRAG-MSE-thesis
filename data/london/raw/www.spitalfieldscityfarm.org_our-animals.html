OUR ANIMALS

 

The farmyard

The farmyard is at the centre of the farm, and home to many much loved animals and great characters. You can see a few of our farm animals below and click on their photos to read about their personalities.

Animal care and welfare is at the heart of everything we do. Our animals help us to educate and increase awareness of the health and welfare issues for pets and food producing animals.

We aim to:

  • Manage the farmyard demonstrating best practice in animal welfare.

  • Promote animal care and welfare.

  • Provide information to explore farming practices past, present, and future.

  • Provide training in animal care and to signpost to further resources.

  • Provide access to animals and provide animals for the community.

Rare breeds survival trust

We are proud to be a RBST approved conservation city farm.

Volunteers take part in the animals daily care and have the opportunity to learn about their needs. Find out more about our volunteering opportunities!
