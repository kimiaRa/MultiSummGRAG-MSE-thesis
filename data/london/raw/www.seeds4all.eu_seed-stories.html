The Seed Gap - How to Cultivate Knowledge for Seed Autonomy? Part 2, From Niche Knowledge to Collective Capacity 

 

Although official trainings largely overlook the strategic role of seeds in agri-food practices, autonomy-based seed knowledge circulates through peer networks. Decades of seed monopolisation by the agricultural and food industries have made the loss of knowledge about seed diversity so pervasive that even its very absence seems to go unnoticed. Yet seed stewardship has always been an essential part of working with agriculture and food. So, how can we challenge the dominant approach to seed practices and bring alternative knowledge to a wider audience? Continue reading...

Where the Untended Feeds Us - Cécile Gilquin's "Tiers-Paysage"

 

Cécile Gilquin was drawn to the concept of Tiers-Paysage that celebrates the neglected — those plants that we too often forget to appreciate — as “biological diversity that has not yet been classified as richness”, while also showing that it is possible to interact with nature without seeking to dominate it. Since launching her project in 2020, she has been cultivating an alternative approach to food — one where biodiversity is free to flourish.
