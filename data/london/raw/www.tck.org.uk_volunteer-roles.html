Ways to Volunteer

At Tooting Community Kitchen, you can volunteer in various capacities, including meal preparation, packaging, distribution, event coordination, and administrative support, ensuring there's a diverse range of opportunities for everyone to contribute to the community's well-being.

  • The Stall

    Make our guests feel welcome providing them with a diverse selection of delicious meals from generous donations by supporting supermarkets, local restaurants, and homemade dishes by our volunteers and students.

  • Become a driver

    Collect and deliver supplies and donations for a cooks and stalls.

  • The Stall - Venn St

    Make our guests feel welcome by providing them with a selection of teas, coffees hot chocolates and snacks.

  • Food Bank Fridays

    Join us at the Friday Foodbank where we get so many incredibly kind and generous donations from our food partners and sponsors. Help pack for our guests as well as serving a range of drinks and snacks.

  • Rebel Club

    Love to meet people? Come join us at scouts hall where we have a range of activities from pilates, craft club, music therapy and the lounge.
