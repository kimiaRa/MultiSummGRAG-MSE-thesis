Welcome to

Tooting Community Kitchen

Our Goal

Our goal is to help alleviate poverty in South West London by helping feed
the homeless and those in need, and providing them with other vital resources.

Our Organization

At Tooting Community Kitchen, we consistently nourish the hungry and accompany the lonely through our weekly community dining events. By serving delicious meals and fostering engaging conversations, we utilize food that might otherwise be wasted, creating a meaningful impact in our community.

Take Action

Join our TCK Family in whatever way works best for you.
We have a range of ways you can help volunteers just take a look at our volunteers roles page.

Find Out How

We aim to act as a practical and emotional support network providing stability to our guests.

Tooting Community Kitchen

  • Volunteer

    We have several different roles from welcoming our guests, cooking up a storm in the kitchen, and helping collect surplus food. All volunteers must be over 18 and no specific experience is required.

  • Donate

    Donations don’t just have to be monetary we love receiving home-cooked meals + baked goods from our volunteers.

    Please note you must be a registered volunteer and have filled out a cookery form.

  • Partner up

    We always love expanding our TCK family with extra partners. Our partners are extremely important and help us accomplish our goals. Please fill out our partner form if you’re interested.
