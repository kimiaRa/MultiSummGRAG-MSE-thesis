Who We Are About Surplus to Supper

Our fleet of vans collect surplus food from businesses that would otherwise end up in landfill.

We redistribute this food to food banks, whilst our kitchen creates nutritious meals for those who need them most in our local community.

We also run a weekly market, and also cater events like weddings and corporate get-togethers with 100% surplus food.

None of this would be possible without our dedicated team and amazing 200+ volunteers and we're still growing!

How we accomplish our mission Our Initiatives

To accomplish our goal of reducing food waste and alleviating food poverty we run the following initiatives:

Food Redistribution

We redistribute surplus food to over 150 local charities, making sure it reaches those in need quickly and efficiently.

More About Food Redistribution

Kitchen

Our kitchen prepares nutritious meals from surplus food, providing delicious and healthy options for our community.

More About Kitchen

Events

Making a difference while celebrating. You can use our catering services for weddings, birthday parties, Christmas parties, and more.

More About Kitchen Events

Our Market

Our market offers fresh produce and groceries at affordable prices, ensuring everyone has access to healthy food. The market helps keep the vans running and the charities fed.

More About Market

Driving Support in the Community Our Volunteers

Volunteers are the driving force behind our operations, from collecting surplus food from local businesses to preparing and distributing meals. Without their dedication, our mission to reduce food waste and combat hunger would be impossible. 

By volunteering with us, you help ensure that perfectly good food reaches those who need it most, rather than ending up in landfills. Whether you can offer a few hours a week or more, every bit of help counts and makes a tangible difference in the lives of those we serve.

Join Us in Fighting Food Waste - Get Involved Today

Making a Difference Every Day Our Impact

Since 2017, we have grown from one van and three collections a week to five vans moving three tonnes of food daily, serving over 150 local charities and providing the equivalent of 3 million meals a year.

Our Charities Who We Support

We support various charities by redistributing surplus food to help alleviate food poverty. These charities, including local shelters, community centers, and food banks, rely on us for consistent and quality food supplies. Through these partnerships, we are able to ensure that surplus food reaches those in need, reducing waste and supporting community welfare. By collaborating with a network of dedicated organisations, we maximise our impact, providing essential resources to vulnerable populations and promoting food sustainability.
