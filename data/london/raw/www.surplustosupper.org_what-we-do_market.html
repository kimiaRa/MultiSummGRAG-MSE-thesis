Welcome to Surplus Market

Fresh Food, Reduced Waste

Our Surplus Market operates every Saturday and Sunday, offering perfectly good food that supermarkets and suppliers have in excess. Fill your shopping bags and make a donation to support our cause.

Join Us Every Weekend About the Market

This is not a foodbank - the Surplus Market is run by volunteers and is open every Saturday and Sunday from 11:30 am to 1:00 pm. The Market enables the local community to support the charity whilst also reducing food waste and food poverty locally.
Every item has been rescued from going to landfill, thanks to the tireless efforts of our volunteers who collect, sort, and make it available at the Market. 
Become a Surplus Shopper and come visit us this weekend and see the difference you can make by simply choosing to shop with Surplus to Supper. We can provide your own Surplus shopping bag and in exchange for a minimum mindful donation you can take away a fantastic range of foods. Together, we can create a more sustainable and caring community.

Shop, Donate, Support How It Works

Fill your shopping bags with a variety of surplus food items. Pay with cash or card. We kindly ask for a mindful donation per bag. Your donations help us keep our vans running and support our community year-round.

By shopping at the Surplus Market, you're not only getting great deals on a variety of food items, but you're also contributing to a larger cause. Your participation helps us fight food waste, reduce environmental impact, and strengthen our community. Join us in making a positive difference—visit us this weekend and see the impact your shopping can have!

Where to find us Visit Us

The market is held every Saturday and Sunday at Sunbury Cricket Club, from 11:30 am to 1:00 pm. We kindly ask you bring your own bags and make a mindful donation per bag (or more if you're feeling generous!).

There is free parking on site and everyone is welcome!
