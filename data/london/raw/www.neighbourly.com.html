We support good causes to generate positive social change

Neighbourly is an award-winning B Corp that helps companies donate resources to local charities and good causes. We believe global change starts locally - one hour, one meal, one donation at a time. We've set a goal to deliver £10 billion of positive impact by 2030.

Visit about us page

How we do it

Neighbourly brings together three key resources - time, money and surplus products - which our corporate partners and local people donate to charitable organisations. These organisations then put these resources to work, creating real social impact and helping their communities thrive.

Volunteering

Neighbourly connects volunteers with over 45,000 local charities across the UK & Ireland. We match volunteers based on community needs, ensuring time and skills create maximum impact.

Financial donations

Neighbourly distributes grant funding through a simple, effective process that helps the right charitable organisations apply for the right grants. We also support direct donations and employee match-giving schemes, making it easy for everyone to give back.

Surplus product redistribution

Whether it's food or non-food products, Neighbourly links surplus items to a nationwide network of local good causes ready to collect from anywhere in the UK. When extra help is needed, the Surplus Saviours programme steps in, with members of the public helping to make sure nothing goes to waste.

Impact reporting

Our impact reports go deeper than “input” numbers. Extractable data helps businesses understand their social and environmental impact across a range of metrics and locations, in easily shareable formats.

Why Neighbourly?

What sets us apart is over 10 years of lasting support for local communities, backed by award-winning service and the ability to deliver national programmes that make a real difference at scale.

Largest network of vetted charities & good causes in the UK

More than 45,000 carefully vetted good causes are ready to receive support, with smart matching technology connecting businesses to the causes that matter most to their teams and customers.

Market leaders in social impact reporting

Our unique calculation engine reveals detailed insights into the social value generated by resource donations, helping to highlight the real impact being made.

Complete giving platform

Neighbourly is the only platform that brings together three vital ways for businesses to give - employee volunteering, financial donations and surplus product redistribution - all in one solution.

Free for charities & good causes, forever

Good causes receive every bit of the resources donated by businesses, with no fees or charges to use the platform.

Cookies on Neighbourly

Cookies on Neighbourly

Neighbourly has a transparent cookie usage policy. Below is a summary, but if you would like more information please visit our Cookies policy page.

We use cookies & data to

  • Allow the website to operate (manage logins/allow videos to play/work within EU GDPR/submit forms etc)
  • Understand how people use the website
  • Improve the website and develop new features
  • Manage network servers to ensure the website has no downtime
  • Assess the success of external advertising and optimise user journeys
  • Allow Neighbourly content to be shared through social media

Things we don't do

  • Sell user data to third parties (Neighbourly is a certified B Corp)
  • Track user behaviour off site
