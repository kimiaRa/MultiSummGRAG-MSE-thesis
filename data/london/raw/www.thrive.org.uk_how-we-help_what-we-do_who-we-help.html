You can at any time change or withdraw your consent from the Cookie Declaration on our website.

Who we help

Hear from some of the client gardeners who attend our programmes across the UK and why they enjoy gardening with Thrive.

We support and work with a wide range of people... those who have a physical or learning disability; people with mental health support needs; people who may have sensory loss or autism; people with age-related conditions such as dementia, heart problems, diabetes or stroke survivors and also young people who have social, emotional or behavioural difficulties.
