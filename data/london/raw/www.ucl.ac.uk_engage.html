Engage with us

Discover the many ways you can connect with UCL, and how we work with industry, government and not-for-profit organisations to tackle tough challenges.

Breadcrumb trail

  • UCL Home
  • Engage with us

Since 1826, we have challenged orthodoxy and applied ourselves to making the world better. We strive to exemplify the strong entrepreneurial spirit and desire for equity and justice that inspired our founding.

Today and looking to the future, we give big plans and brave ideas the scale and the support they need to succeed.

Explore below how we collaborate to make positive change happen across knowledge sharing and partnerships, alumni and benefactors and communities and outreach.

Contact us, we look forward to engaging with you.
