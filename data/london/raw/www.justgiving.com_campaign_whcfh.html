Story

The West Hampstead Community Food Hub, based at Sidings Community Centre, provides food (including fresh fruit & veg) to over 140 local families each Saturday. The Food Hub started at the height of the Covid19 pandemic, when many of our neighbours reached out to the West Hampstead & Kilburn Mutual Aid Groups for food support due to ill health, disability or unemployment.

We originally planned to support 70 families in the NW6 / NW2 area each week and that number has continued to grow.

To sustain our current numbers we need to raise equivalent of £1000 each week. We would love your support to achieve that.

On this page you can make a one-off donation, or you can visit The Sidings Community Centre page to make a monthly donation, which would go a long way to helping us ensure we are sustainable: https://www.justgiving.com/sidingscc (all monthly donations made on the Sidings page will be flagged for the Food Hub).

- £3 per week would buy fresh fruit & veg for a household

- £25 per month would support one household every other week

- £50 per month would support one household each week

Together, we can make a difference to our neighbours in need. Please be part of our supportive community and give what you can to the West Hampstead Community Food Hub.

To find out more, or to see our drop off locations for food donations, visit our website here: https://westhampsteadcommunityfoodhub.org/ or follow us @whampfoodhub on Twitter & Instagram.

Thank you.
