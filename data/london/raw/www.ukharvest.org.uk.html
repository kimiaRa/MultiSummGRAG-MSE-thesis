We are on a mission to reduce food waste increase food security by enriching and educating our communities.

UKHarvest rescues surplus food to prevent it from going to waste

We collect food that would have gone to landfill from across the food chain and make sure it is available to those in need and those wishing to learn about cooking, storing and saving money through free food education, NOURISHed. 

The food is distributed throughout our community, to schools and to local charities

Our fleet of refrigerated vans bring the rescued surplus food to our warehouse where we sort and redistribute it.

Rescued Food is used to feed those in need 

Our rescued food is redistributed through our Community Food Hubs, Grub Clubs, Community Grub Clubs, Nourish Hub and Nourish Hub Cafe, giving people in need access to affordable healthy food and teaching them how to make the most of it.

About Us

UKHarvest is a not-for-profit perishable food rescue operation and education charity, eliminating hunger and food waste. We support local charities by rescuing food that would have gone to waste and deliver it directly to charities.
