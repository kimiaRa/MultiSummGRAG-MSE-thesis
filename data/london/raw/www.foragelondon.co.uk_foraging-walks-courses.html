Welcome to Forage London and Beyond, a collective of independent foraging teachers offering London wild food walks, Dorset seashore and coastal foraging, Hampshire and New Forest mushroom hunting.

Foraging Walks & Courses

If you don’t find what you are looking for please join a waiting list and check back another day. More foraging walks & courses coming soon. Before booking please read our  T&Cs and Cancellation Policy

I do my best to make these foraging courses good value, to pay the tutors decent fees, location costs, insurance etc. I’m aware that the end result makes them, for some people, prohibitively expensive so If you’re low waged or can’t afford to attend then email me, hello@foragelondon.co.uk and we can discuss concessions, a discount or skill swap.
