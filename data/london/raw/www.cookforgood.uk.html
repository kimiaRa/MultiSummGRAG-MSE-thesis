Get involved
Cook for Good is a social enterprise that’s tackling food insecurity, social isolation and barriers to work on a social housing estate in Kings Cross.
We run team building activities in our kitchen and elsewhere, making huge quantities of food to share with local residents and outreach organisations.
And we use the profits to fund a programme which includes free cooking courses, training and employment programmes, and a surplus food pantry.
Cook for Good is a social enterprise that’s tackling food insecurity, social isolation and barriers to work on a social housing estate in Kings Cross.
We run team cooking experiences in our shiny new kitchen, making huge quantities of food to share with local residents and outreach organisations.
And we use the profits to fund a community programme which includes free cooking classes, employment programmes and a surplus food pantry.

Business Gives Back

Around 350,000 London families are facing food insecurity, having to cut back on essential foods, or skip meals completely. Will you join our Business Gives Back campaign and help us create 5000 family meals to share? Just book a Family Meal Box Challenge between 1 June and 31 August.

Cook for Change

In 2025 we launched Cook for Change, a seven-month programme designed to support founders and directors of community food enterprises to learn from our model and become income-generating. Read about how the programme unfolded and what our first cohort achieved.

Available now: Soup for Good

Soup for Good is a beautifully produced book of recipes and stories from the Cook for Good community. Explore our favourite soups, no-stress breads and delicious toppings, and meet some of Priory Green’s much-loved characters. All profits will be used to tackle food insecurity and social isolation.

Explore the book

Extra sessions at our surplus food pantry

Due to overwhelming demand from our community, we're now offering morning and evening Pantry sessions. Members pay £3.50 a week, which entitles them to come in and shop for a basket of fresh, frozen and storecupboard ingredients (worth between £30 and £35).

For businesses

Our corporate team building activities are the most fun you can have in the kitchen, combining hot pans, cool tunes and the chance to give something back. Roll up your sleeves and get stuck in, working as a team to cook up to 100 meals to share with our community – and enough for yourselves to eat, too.

For the community

Come and be part of the Cook for Good family, and get food, support and training on your doorstep. Whether you’d like to take part in a cooking course, choose a weekly basket of low-cost food, or gain skills and experience to help you into work, there’s a whole load of opportunities waiting here for you.
