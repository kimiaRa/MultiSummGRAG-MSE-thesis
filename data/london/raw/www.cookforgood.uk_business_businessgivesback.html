This summer, we’re fighting back against the hunger that’s affecting families across London, by creating and sharing 5000 meals.

So we’re asking businesses to get a team together and take on a Family Meal Box Challenge between 1 June and 31 August. Why not use your summer team social or volunteer day to give something back?

Give back to Londoners through a Family Meal Box Challenge

Around 350,000 London households are experiencing food insecurity, having to cut back on essential foods, or skip meals completely. And as healthier food such as fruit and veg has been shown to cost twice as much as unhealthy options, their health is being hit, too.

We’re tackling this head on this summer, challenging corporate teams to create Hello Fresh style healthy meal boxes, which we’ll share with our own community and others across London, supported by The Felix Project. Last year, we provided 2400 meals for local families, so this time we’re going bigger and better with a 5000 meal target. Are you ready to take on a Family Meal Box Challenge?

Any size team, anywhere that suits you

Our high-energy Family Meal Box Challenge events are a brilliant mix of teamwork and friendly competition, with fun and purpose baked in. Perfect for a summer get-together or volunteer day, they’re a powerful way to build team connection whilst making a tangible difference.

After a quick team briefing, we’ll split you into sub teams and get you peeling, chopping, slicing and dicing a mountain of meal ingredients, which you’ll then package up ready to share. There will be awards for teams that really smash it, with quality and team spirit as important as speed.

We can host up to 70 people in our spaces, or any number at your offices or another off-site venue. The campaign is running between 1 June and 31 August – now all we need is you.

Are you in?
