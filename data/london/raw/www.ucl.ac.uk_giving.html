it will happen
Here, is a university that defied a king, refused to accept the established order and opened the doors to education wider.

Breadcrumb trail

  • UCL Home
  • Here, it will happen

Our founding vision was made real through philanthropy. That spirit of free thinking and open enquiry still shines brightly at UCL today. We remain a place where breadth of learning meets the brilliance of our people. And where values of openness and inclusivity – championed by our spiritual founder Jeremy Bentham – guide how we turn ideas into solutions that shape society.

Here and now, we continue to open doors for people from all backgrounds. One in four of our UK undergraduate students are from households with low-incomes – proof that talent, not circumstance, still defines success at UCL. 

But we can and must do more.

The UCL Student Support Fund

Here, is where students, academics and educators from across the sciences, arts and humanities gather in one of the world’s great cities. Where philosophical leaps, medical advances, technological breakthroughs, cosmic discoveries, world-changing innovations, and creative insights continue to happen each and every day. 

But to make all this happen, we will need your help.
