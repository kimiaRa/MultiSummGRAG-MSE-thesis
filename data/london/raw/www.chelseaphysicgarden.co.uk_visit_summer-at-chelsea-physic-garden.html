Summer starts here

Since 1673, Chelsea Physic Garden has been a place to explore, learn about and marvel at nature’s wonders. Home to nearly 5000 medicinal, edible and useful plants, the Garden has been an outdoor classroom for over 350 years. With a full programme of events, from botanical workshops to al fresco dining, children’s theatre to live music performances, this summer is the perfect time to visit.

Open Sunday to Friday, you can join a free guided tour, stop by the shop and café, or simply enjoy these long summer days in an oasis of green, located only 15 minutes from Sloane Square or Victoria Station.

Open: Sunday – Friday, 11am – 5pm.
Tickets: £5 – £15.

Make the most of the warmer weather and spend it in our secluded four-acre walled garden in the heart of Chelsea. Surrounded by nature with the sounds of birds and busy pollinators, relax during the evening at Chelsea Physic Garden this summer.

Summer at Chelsea Physic Garden

Experience summer in full bloom this year at Chelsea Physic Garden. Soak up the sun, listen to the birds, enjoy an ice cold drink and explore London’s oldest botanic garden. We’re open Sunday to Friday, from 11am to 5pm. With you admission ticket, you get access to a free guided tour, access to the historic glasshouses and all day to explore this four-acre oasis of calm and plants.

Throughout August we’ll be open late on Wednesdays, so the perfect opportunity to take time out after a busy day with friends or by yourself.

Visit after hours and enjoy live music on the lawn, bring a picnic and get a drink from the bar for an evening experience you won’t forget. Or if food is more your thing, book on one of our Supper Clubs for a night of fine dining hosted by guest chefs.

Make the most of the warmer weather and spend it in our secluded four-acre walled garden in the heart of Chelsea. Surrounded by nature with the sounds of birds and busy pollinators, relax during the evening at Chelsea Physic Garden this summer.

Supper Clubs

The Physic Garden Café would like to welcome you to a series of evenings that speaks to all your senses, taste, smells and sight. It is a celebration of culture and nature accompanied by delicious food.

These Supper Clubs will be hosted in the midst of the Garden and you will have the magic of dining under the stars.

Book yourself a seat at the next Supper Club After Hours and get the chance to experience the culinary world of Chelsea Physic Garden.

The evening will start at 7pm with a chilled Aperitivo followed by a seasonal set tasting menu seated in the Garden. You will have the opportunity to meet the Chef behind your plate while dining.

Considering the dining will be outdoor in the evening we do recommend to bring a jumper or jacket along with you. In case of bad weather, you will be seated inside the Café or under our marquee area.
