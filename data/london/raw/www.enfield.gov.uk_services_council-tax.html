    Council Tax

    Important notice

    If you have any questions about your Council Tax bill, please see our frequently asked questions.

    Enfield residents are charged Council Tax for the range of services offered by the council. Use this section of our website to access information about Council Tax, such as how much you need to pay and who is liable. You can set up a Direct Debit to make monthly payments, and apply for discounts and exemptions. You can also read our Council Tax guide for detailed information about the current financial year.
