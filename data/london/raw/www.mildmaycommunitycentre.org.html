Join Our Team of Volunteers

Be Part of Something Brilliant in Mildmay

Do you want to make a real difference where you live?

Mildmay Community Partnership is building a vibrant team of volunteers who help strengthen community life across the Mildmay area and we’d love you to be part of it.

You don’t need any previous experience.

Just bring your enthusiasm, your ideas, and your passion for the neighbourhood.

Get involved in ways that suit you

  • Events and community celebrations
  • Gardening and outdoor projects
  • Foodhub & Community Kitchen
  • Or even stepping into a leadership role as a Trustee >>>

Whether you have an hour a week or a few days/evenings a month, there’s a place for you here…

Join Our Board of Trustees

Help Shape the Future of Mildmay

We’re always looking for new Trustees to join Mildmay Community Partnership and support our mission to strengthen community life in the Mildmay area.

If you care about your neighbourhood and want to make a meaningful impact, this is a brilliant opportunity to get involved.

You don’t need previous board experience.

We welcome people of all ages, backgrounds and experiences especially those who live, work, study or volunteer in the Mildmay ward. 

Skills we’re particularly interested in:

  • Charities
  • Community work
  • Fundraising 
  • Finance
  • Human Resources
  • Legal
