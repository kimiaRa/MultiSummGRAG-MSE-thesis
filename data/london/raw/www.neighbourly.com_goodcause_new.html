Matomo

We use Matomo to collect information about visitor behaviour on our website. These are cookies that allow us to recognise and count the number of visitors and to better understand the movement of our users on the website.

This analytics data is not tied to personally identifiable information. We do not collect or store your personal information, therefore, this information cannot be used to identify you. To find out more about Matomo's privacy policy and its analytics service, please view our Privacy policy
