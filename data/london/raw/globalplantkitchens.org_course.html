This free course contains modules to support you to start a plant-based community kitchen. Developed by Made In Hackney, sharing a decade of knowledge, insight and learning.

Access the topics below that you are most interested in. If you don’t know where to start check out our 10 steps to start a plant-based community kitchen.

These resources are for community food projects – and people looking to start one – anywhere in the world. We are here to help. If you have any questions or can’t find what you are looking for please contact us at Jacqueline@madeinhackney.org
