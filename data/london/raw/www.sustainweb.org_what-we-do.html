Working with our members and supporters, we campaign for a healthy and sustainable food system, which is publicly accountable and socially and environmentally responsible. Our campaigns and projects fall into the following areas of work:

Food governance

Sustainable farming

Good food economy

Good food for all

All our projects & campaigns

Consultancy

Sustain helps local authorities and public health teams drive local change in food policy and practice. We provide advice, research and strategy development to help you transform local food systems alongside a wealth of insights through our food and farming networks, including national, local government and place-based organisations.

Find out more

Diversity outreach

Sustain's diversity outreach programme seeks to centre racial justice and strengthen ethnic diversity representation across the sustainable food and farming sector.
