education workshops

We offer interactive and engaging workshops on a range of topics to do with food, farming and the natural world.

Here are some of the most popular workshops we get asked to deliver. If you have a topic not covered here, let us know and we can usually meet your needs.

All workshops are £100 for an hour and a half with an experienced member of the Farm’s Education Team. Maximum capcity for each workshop varies so please check in the workshop description that it will meet your class size.

After the workshop you are welcome to stay and explore the Farm and its gardens.

Animal handling

Ever thought about what it takes to look after farm animals?

Under the guidance of the Farm’s Livestock Team learn about each animal, their behaviour and lifestyle.

Each student will get an opportunity to work one-on-one with the animals, including close-up contact, grooming and leading on halters.
Find out more

Chicken and egg

By looking at the life-cycle of the chicken we learn about how a chick develops inside the egg, hatches out and then grows into a full grown clucking, pecking chicken in the yard.

Introduces concepts around biological development and where our food comes from.

Habitat

What do plants and animals need to survive?

We explore the various habitats around the farm both wild and domestic, and discover the wonderful range of creatures that live in them.
Book workshop

Work experience

At Hackney City Farm we offer a rare opportunity for students living in the city to experience what it is like working on a farm.

We have a range of work experience opportunities in animal husbandry, horticulture and environmental management for students considering a career outdoors.

Places are limited so please get in touch to arrange your placement well in advance of the date.

Apply for work experience

garden

Open all year round to the public, the garden is divided into sections: the orchard area with apple, pear and plum trees, and the bog garden filled with wetland plants. A large part of the garden is given over to raised beds in which we grow vegetables and at the top end of the garden, we have a selection of herb beds with many different varieties including types of mint, sage, thyme and marjoram.

Pottery

We have pottery classes for children and adults. The sessions are drop in so no need to book.

Our resident potters will help you design and make your own clay objects and the price includes firing in the kiln. We have only two pottery wheels so you will not be able to spend a whole class using a wheel. It takes up to 3 weeks to get your piece fired for collection.

Children’s pottery – £10/£5

11:30–13:00 £10 per child

14:00–15:30 £10 per child

Adult pottery – £20
Wednesdays 19:00–21:00.
Thurdays 19:00–21:00.

Psychotherapy

Gaia Therapy Project is a low cost psychotherapy and counselling service offering weekly therapy sessions to individuals for up to one year.

Gaia provides affordable psychotherapeutic services to those who need them most. We aspire to cultivate a community and culture in which people feel welcome and able to explore the challenges of living.

Choir

Come and sing as the Hackney Harmony, a diverse and vibrant choir in the heart of East London. We sing a wide range of music including Gospel, African, Folk, Eastern European, Sacred songs, and pop. We have regular concerts as well as gigs at local events and venues such as The Nest Collective, Woodburner Folk night, and Shoreditch Church.
