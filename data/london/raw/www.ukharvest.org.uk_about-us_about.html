About us

UKHarvest provides access to affordable food, reduces food waste and increases food security. 

We collect surplus edible food, free of charge, from any organisations that have surplus food to donate.  We stop it being thrown away and make sure it reaches the homes of those in need and those wishing to reduce food waste. 

Our fleet of vans, drivers, staff and volunteers work across the community, offering support through Community Food Hubs, Community Grub Clubs and Grub Clubs.

We operate our Community Food Hub in London, Nourish Hub and more recently our Community Cafe in Bognor, Nourish Hub Cafe. 

No one wants to waste their own money by throwing food away at home, so UKHarvest uses the rescued food and works closely with our chefs, educators and nutritionists, to create and deliver our free food education programme, NOURISHed. NOURISHed creates resilience in the kitchen, helps to reduce food waste at home and helps everyone to eat and live well while saving money.
