Empowering people to grow food and create healthy ecosystems. We do this together through community, education and forest gardening.

 

 

 

 

WELCOME

 

EDIBLE LANDSCAPES LONDON is a not-for-profit community education project. We specialise in forest gardening, a design system which creates healthy ecosystems. At the same time, it empowers us to grow our own food, medicines and materials, benefitting both wildlife and humans. Volunteer sessions take place at our site twice a week, among other activities at our site, and within the park.

Opening times – volunteer days

 

We are open to the public every Tuesday 10am – 3pm, and Sundays 10am – 2pm.

On these days we host volunteer sessions in the garden. On other days of the week the garden is open to different groups and workshops.

*Please note, our Sundays are volunteer-led. To confirm these sessions in advance, contact us to join our Whatsapp Group.

Our volunteering page gives information about these drop-in sessions.

Forest Explorers

 

Our weekly Forest Explorers group provides relaxed drop-in stay and play for parents/ carers and little ones led by qualified Forest School Leader Aurora, every Wednesday from 10-12pm in the coldest months and 10-1pm the rest of the year.

 Activities are primarily aimed at the early years stage 0-5 years, but children of all ages are welcome to join and respectfully explore the garden.

We encourage child led open ended play and sensory exploration of the seasons and the natural world through touch, taste, feel and imagination.

 The sessions are completely free and do not require booking. Participants are welcome to drop in and out at anytime.
