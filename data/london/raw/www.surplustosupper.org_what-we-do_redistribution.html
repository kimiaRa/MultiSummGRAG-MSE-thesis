Food Redistribution

Delivering Surplus Food to Those in Need

The Redistribution arm of Surplus to Supper collects surplus food for free and delivers it to local charities, ensuring it reaches those who need it most.

Feeding Communities, Reducing Waste

Feeding Communities, Reducing Waste
Undertaking evening collections and early morning deliveries, our volunteers are the driving force behind our mission to reduce food waste and food poverty locally.
We operate a fleet of vans—staffed entirely by volunteers—that collect surplus food from local businesses and deliver it to charities, drop-in centres, dementia organisations, and food banks within a 10-mile radius. This ensures fresh, high-quality food reaches vulnerable individuals and families quickly and efficiently.
Every day, our volunteers sort and distribute rescued food to our partner organisations, supporting a wide network of community services. Whether it’s a local food bank, a school or a community project, or a specialised charity, our goal is simple: no good food should go to waste, and everyone deserves access to culturally relevant nutritious meals.
Thanks to our incredible volunteer team, we’re building a more sustainable, compassionate community—one delivery at a time.

Want to Help? Volunteer

Volunteering with us in our food redistribution efforts is a rewarding way to make a significant impact in your community. As a volunteer, you can help with collecting surplus food from local businesses, sorting and packing food items, and delivering them to various charities and food banks. Your dedication and time ensure that fresh, nutritious food reaches those in need, reducing food waste and supporting our mission. Whether you have a few hours a week or more to spare, every bit of help makes a difference. Don't just take our word for it—watch this video to hear from our current volunteers about their experiences and the positive change they've helped create. Join us today and be a part of this vital and fulfilling work!
