No Garden?

No Problem!

👩‍🌾 Garden Tenders

Live in a flat, or need more room to grow? If you’re keen to grow your own food, flowers, learn new skills, or simply spend more time in nature — connect with someone happy to share their garden space. Helping with garden chores is welcome — but not essential. Every arrangement is unique and must suit both parties.

Why Tend a Garden?
✓ Grow fruit, veg, flowers — brilliant for your well-being
✓ Connect with a local garden owner
✓ Learn or share gardening skills
✓ Be part of a kind, supported Patch-Matching process

Whether you're just starting out or a seasoned grower without space, find a garden to grow in, and a community to grow with. 🌸

Can’t Garden?

Find out who can.

🏡 Garden Lenders

Got a garden that needs a little love? Share your space with someone who’s eager to grow — you could help a budding gardener bloom. Sharing gardening skills is welcome, but not essential. You could even share produce grown, but every arrangement must be based on what suits both parties.

Why Lend Your Garden?
✓ Connect with like-minded, green-fingered people
✓ Enjoy the potential for extra help in the garden
✓ Benefit from our supported, thoughtful Patch-Matching process

Someone could love your garden as much as you. Your garden could mean the world to someone without one. Let’s grow something good together. 🌱

Garden Sharing Registration – One-Off Payment
Join the Lend and Tend community with a one-time payment of £12. This supports your place on the waiting list and helps cover admin and Patch-Matching costs when a local garden-sharing opportunity arises.

No subscriptions, no hidden fees — just a one-off contribution to help us grow connections, community, and green spaces with care. 🌿

How Lend and Tend works

1️⃣ Join & Register

Sign up for £12, you’ll fill in the form to register. We’ll pin your location to the map and you’ll then be on the waiting list.

2️⃣ Get Matched

Should a potential Patch Match become apparent, you’ll be contacted if a suitable person wants to contact you to garden share.

3️⃣ Start Growing

We’ll make introductions and can support with arrangements like agreements, then you’ll begin your garden sharing journey.
