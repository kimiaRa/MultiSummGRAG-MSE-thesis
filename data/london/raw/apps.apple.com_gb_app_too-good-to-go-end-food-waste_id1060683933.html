    Too Good To Go is your smart way to enjoy tasty food at great value, while doing good for the planet. Winner of the 2023 ‘Cultural Impact’ category at the prestigious Apple App Store Awards, the #1 app to reduce food waste helps you save delicious, unsold snacks, meals, and ingredients from local shops, cafés, supermarkets, restaurants, and top brands—all at great prices. In a world where 40% of food produced goes to waste each year, reducing food waste is the #1 action we can take to help tackle climate change. With Too Good To Go, you can unlock affordable meals and grocery items while helping to save the planet. Together, we have the power to make a real difference. HOW TOO GOOD TO GO WORKS: EXPLORE AND DISCOVER Download the app to explore a map showing nearby restaurants, cafés, bakeries, supermarkets or trusted brands offering good food at a great price, ready to be rescued. SAVE YOUR SURPRISE BAG OR TOO GOOD TO GO PARCEL Browse a variety of Surprise Bags filled with tasty, unsold food—whether it’s sushi, pizza, burgers, or fresh fruits and veggies. Prefer to get your favourite food brands delivered to you? Save a Too Good To Go Parcel packed with good food from the brands you love, at great prices. AFFORDABLE EATS Save a Surprise Bag or a Too Good To Go Parcel at ½ price or less. RESERVE YOUR SAVE Confirm your purchase through the app to reserve your Surprise Bag and rescue these delicious meals from going to waste. By rescuing food, you save money and help fight food waste. ENJOY Collect your Surprise Bag at the scheduled time, or have your Too Good To Go Parcel delivered directly to you. WHY TOO GOOD TO GO?: WALLET-FRIENDLY INDULGENCE Enjoy quality meals at affordable prices, satisfying both your taste buds and your wallet. VARIETY AND CHOICE Too Good To Go partners with a wide selection of local favourites and top brands, offering everything from sushi, pizza, baked and fresh goods to easy-to-store staple grocery items such as snacks, drinks, sweets or pasta. ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT Each meal saved avoids CO2e emissions and the unnecessary use of water and land resources. By rescuing food from going to waste, you take a step towards a greener, cleaner planet. EASY PURCHASE PROCESS The app’s simple and user-friendly interface makes it easy to browse, choose, and save Surprise Bags or Too Good To Go Parcels. CONVENIENCE Pick up your Surprise Bag at the designated time, or have a Too Good To Go Parcel delivered directly to you. JOIN THE TOO GOOD TO GO COMMUNITY Join the community of food lovers making a positive impact on the environment. Download the app now and start reducing food waste, bite by bite. Reducing food waste is a key action to help tackle climate change. For more information, visit tgtg.to/claims

    • Editors’ Choice

      Food waste is one of the great challenges of our time, Too Good to Go is one way to contribute to a solution. The app provides an overview of shops and restaurants in your area that have surplus food. Those outlets put together a surprise bag at a lower price and you can order it right within the app. This way you can protect the environment and discover new places at the same time.

    • Great Idea and Reason

      23/04/2025

      PiperMantra

      Such an ethical approach to food that’s good for the planet and helps to avoid food wastage. If you are on a low budget, this is a good way to find and have a good ready-made meal. You will also in the process find eateries and stores where you particularly like the quality and variety of what they have to offer. You must strictly be at the establishment within the timezone given. I’ve probably been using this app for around 3 years. There were some teething problems with it earlier on, such as a restaurant being closed when I turned up to collect the food a few times. Also, the page to show proof to the establishment that you paid for the food, and the swiper you have to use, completely disappeared a few times, upon arrival at the venue. But more recently, it’s been much more glitch-free. Although a few days ago, I was unable to pay for the item I had selected in the app, as a circle just kept spinning around, I suspect that it was just temporary teething problems. The day after, it worked fine, and I noticed that there were a few updates and a few more questions that asked about my experience at the establishment. Overall, it's highly recommended.
