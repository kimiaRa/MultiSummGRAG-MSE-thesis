Love Where You Live

We make sustainability second nature.

The climate and nature crises are increasingly evident across the UK. Whilst most people want to see change, we're still not seeing it at the scale or speed that's needed.  

Hubbub is changing that. We design real-world projects that prove what works on the ground. We share that evidence with business and government, to give them the confidence to make bolder choices.

Every project is sparked by our passion to problem-solve and connect with people. From helping people get more plants on their plates, to changing the way they heat their homes, we’re proving that environmental action is politically possible, publicly supported and rooted in communities across the UK.

In doing so, we’re supporting bold climate and nature action to become a shared national mission. We’re making sustainability second nature.

Influencing culture

In this information age, some of the most important environmental issues can go unnoticed. We make them hard to ignore with thought-provoking campaigns that have a playful twist. Always positive and intriguing, they reach the mainstream and create a collective appetite for change. 

Our projects inspired an action for the environment every 9 seconds last year alone. We’ve helped Halloween become as much about eating pumpkins as carving them, designed ‘What’s in My Wash?’ to wring out the truth on microplastics from our clothes, and launched a new kind of fishing to clean up our waterways, to name just a few.

    A behaviour-change campaign helping people make simple, sustainable food switches, starting with what’s in their cup.
  • Eat Your Pumpkin

    Scaring off food waste with one simple message, eat your pumpkin.
  • Take it outside

    Take it outside – you can paint, cook, stretch, play and more in your local community garden. Meet new people and breathe in nature, no green thumb necessary.

Changing habits

Humans like habits, which means one of our toughest challenges is changing behaviours. It takes some serious insight-mining, creative thinking and experimenting but once underway these positive changes often spill over to other areas of our lives.

We've had some real wins in encouraging more environmental habits and making them stick. From convincing commuters to change to plant-based milk and getting football fans switching meat for plants, to Just Eat customers cutting food waste with in-app hacks, and supporting UK households to find easy, affordable, greener ways of living. 

So far, we've inspired 10,339 people to transform their way of living for the long-term. In 2025, we inspired an action for the environment every 9 seconds. 
  • Home Advantage

    Developing a blueprint for supporting households to take climate action.
  • Live LAGOM

    Embracing the Swedish philosophy of ‘just the right amount’, we worked with IKEA and their customers to explore how a community could support each other to save money, live sustainably and make positive changes to their home.
  • In Our Nature

    A city-wide collaboration in Manchester that's putting local communities at the heart of climate action.

Resilient communities

Our projects have social benefits too. We reach the mainstream and connect with  underrepresented communities who are affected most yet often heard least. By listening to and including people from all walks of life, we create solutions together that are good for the environment and everyone. 

We’ve cut food waste and created social spaces at the same time through the Community Fridge Network, regifted smartphones to extend the life of tech and reconnect people who were digitally isolated, and helped locals reclaim unloved communal spaces to grow, play, and have access to nature. We’re just getting started.

    Bringing people together to share food, meet up, learn new skills and prevent fresh food from going to waste.
  • Community Calling

Innovation

We experiment, trial, learn, trial, succeed – repeat. For us, we aim to innovate in everything we do, and it doesn’t have to be high-tech. It’s about reimagining how we think, behave and work, and what we create – like turning cigarette butts into ballots to cut litter, riding Gen-Z trends to reduce e-waste, and funding future ideas through innovative and collaborative grant funds.

  • Ballot Bins

    Liam or Noel? Dogs or Cats? Our voting ashtray has proven to cut cigarette litter by up to 73%.
  • Time After Time

    Electrical waste is the fastest growing waste stream in the world and many of us are unsure of the value of keeping items in use or how to repair and recycle them. With VMO2 we’re exploring ways to make positive choices around electrical items, time after time.
  • Eat It Up Fund

    We’re offering six transformative grants of up to £60,000 each to organisations with ground-breaking ideas to cut down on food waste.
