Thank you for your donation!

Want to take climate action?

We are an environmental charity working on issues that connect women, health, equity and environmental justice.

We support women  and communities to take action, amplifying racialised and marginalised women’s voices, and advocate for change.

With your help, we can build a world where women, communities and the planet thrive. If you can, please consider supporting us with a regular gift or one-off donation to support our work.
