GET TOXIC CHEMICALS OUT OF PERIOD PRODUCTS

Add your voice to the global movement demanding safe period products.

SIGN NOW!

JOIN THE GLOBAL MOVEMENT

 Wen’s petition with Pesticide Action Network UK and EKO IS backed by 72,000+ people and a coalition of NGOs, academics and brands, calling for regulation across all period products – together we can’t be ignored!

 

WE ARE CALLING FOR:

Mandatory safety testing of ALL period products.

Full ingredient disclosure by manufacturers.

Legislation to ban toxic chemicals and heavy metals in period products.

 

SIGN NOW!

 

 

TO: UK Prime Minister, Keir Starmer; UK Chemicals Minister, Emma Hardy

 

We urge you to take immediate action to get toxic chemicals out of period products and ensure all period products in the UK are safe to use.

SIGN NOW!

BLOOD SWEAT AND PESTICIDES

Our report with Pesticides Action Network UK found toxic glyphosate in period products at *40 times* higher than the legal limit for drinking water.

Glyphosate is linked to cancer, dementia, fertility issues, and more – and it’s especially dangerous if it’s found in tampons because absorption can be up to 80% higher than other areas of the body.

LEADING THE CAMPAIGN FOR CHANGE

LEADING THE WAY

For 35 years,Wen has led the global movement for safe, sustainable and affordable period products for every woman, girl and person who menstruates.

 

URGENT REGULATION

It’s encouraging to see some period product producers speaking out. This shows how urgent the need for regulation is – but we must make sure the Government hears from everyone.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

leading the change

That’s why Wen is leading the campaign for change, bringing together people, organisations and brands to show the strength of support. In Environmenstrual Week of Action (20–26 October), we’ll hand the petition to the Government. This is the UK’s chance to be a world leader on period product safety. The more support we gather now, the stronger our case.
