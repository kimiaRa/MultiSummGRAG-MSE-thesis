    Community hubs

    Community hubs is a face-to-face service that connects residents with support across 4 pillars:

    Help with money:

    • Refer residents facing financial difficulties to welfare support services
    • Provide debt advice and Council Tax support
    • Aim to stabilize income and prevent further financial distress

    Help with health and wellbeing:

    • Connect residents to health programmes and initiatives
    • Promote health drop-ins for on-the-spot health testing
    • Encourage participation in social support groups like Coffee & Chat to reduce isolation and improve mental well-being

    Help with jobs and skills:

    • Link residents to training programmes that lead to stable employment
    • Offer ESOL classes to improve English language skills and expand job opportunities

    Help with housing stability:

    • Support residents at risk of eviction with financial and wellbeing guidance
    • Connect them to housing advisory services to prevent eviction and homelessness

    Opening times

    The community hubs at Edmonton Green and Enfield Town libraries are open from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday:

    • 9am to 5pm – customers are welcome to self-serve and use the computers and phone booths available
    • 9am to 2:30pm – there is a drop-in service for those who need more help
    • 2:30pm to 5pm – staff will follow up with customers who need more support, either in person, by email, or over the phone

    Locations

    • Edmonton Community Hub – Edmonton Green Library, 36-44 South Mall, Edmonton, N9 0TN
    • Enfield Community Hub – Enfield Town Library, 66 Church Street, Enfield, EN2 6AX

    You can also get help with scanning documents to send to council services if you cannot do it yourself at any of the 4 hub libraries:

    • Edmonton Green
    • Enfield Town
    • Ordnance Unity Centre
    • Palmers Green
