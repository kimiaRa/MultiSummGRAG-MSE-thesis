Worrall and Fuller Recruiting Governors The Worrall and Fuller Exhibition Fund (Charity number 312507) is recruiting additional members to its Board of Governors. Dating back to 1689, we are a small charity that supports the education and training of children and young people who live in, study in or have parents working in, the southern part of Islington – the former London Borough of Finsbury. We make grants, ranging from about £250 – £750 each, to children and young people each year for educational and vocational purpose. Our current priorities are to help meets the cost of extra-curricular, non-mainstream education and out-of-school activities. St Luke’s Centre provides us with a Clerk for the charity, and administrative support. We have entered an exciting stage in the life of the charity, as we have recently received a significant boost to our financial resources arising from a land sale. This will increase the number and size of the grants that we are able to make. Ideally as a Governor you would: • have experience of investment and financial management, especially in the non- profit sector • be living in our area of operation, or have other personal connections with it • have an understanding of state education • support the Chair in the current period of transition and growth • be able to attend four Board meetings (usually on a Tuesday morning) a year and be available from time to time for other consultations The Governors’ positions are unpaid. Asian Cooking Academy Introducing our Room Hire clients: Asian Cooking Academy We’re thrilled that the Asian Cooking Academy, a London-based independent cooking school championing Asian flavours through hands-on masterclasses, team-building experiences, and bespoke cooking events, has booked to run classes and workshops at St Luke’s Cookery School in 2026. Born in Butterworth, Penang and based in London. He has authored several cookbooks, including the bestseller Amazing Malaysian. Norman has appeared on TV shows such as MasterChef Malaysia, BBC’s Best Ever Dishes, and Sunday Brunch, and runs masterclasses, supper clubs, and food events across Europe. Domestic abuse awareness with the Multicultural Women’s Group We are so pleased to announce our new project with the Multicultural Women’s Group: a leaflet on domestic abuse awareness. Women from the group, including those with lived experience of domestic abuse, thought of the idea after attending the Million Women Rise March earlier this year; stressing the need for awareness on this important topic. The group helped design the leaflet, alongside a member of St Luke’s Community Engagement Team. The Multicultural Women’s Group now supports the team to distribute the flyer during school pick-up times and also delivering them through local letterboxes and to other community groups and settings. The women play a key role in identifying the estates, community spaces, schools and places of worship where they recommend we distribute the flyers and promote the initiative.  This timing too is essential as we are aware that the Christmas period often sees a rise in domestic abuse. The flyer that we will share within our diverse south Islington community and includes: Local domestic abuse support organisations, their contact details and a brief description of their services Emergency contact numbers A brief explanation of what constitutes domestic abuse, which the women will help with translations to share in their community networks On Friday 6th February 2026, we held an event to celebrate the success of the leaflet, and how far it had reached. We celebrated on the day by distributing the leaflet around our community to 23 different estates and businesses. In the evening we had a small ‘thank you’ to all the women and staff that helped create this project: We would like to give a big ‘thank you’ to the Mayor of London Office for Policing and Crime for kindly funding this project, the staff that worked on creating and distributing this piece; and finally to the St Luke’s Multicultural Women’s Group – an inspiring and brilliant group of local women!
