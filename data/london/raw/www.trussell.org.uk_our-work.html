Our work

We should all be free from hunger.

We’re an anti-poverty charity and community of food banks. We work together to ensure no one in the UK needs a food bank to survive, while providing emergency support for people left without enough money to live on. 

We need urgent action on hunger in the UK. People are being pushed to the brink because they don’t have enough money to live on. This can’t go on.  

We are 1,400 food bank locations, supported by 40,000 volunteers, 12,000 churches, 100,000s of community groups and schools, and millions of people around the UK.  

We work locally and across the UK for a more just and compassionate society where no one needs a food bank to survive. We’re determined to make this a reality.  

Until this happens, we provide emergency food and practical support to people in their hardest moments. And work with our partners and local communities to ensure everyone gets the right help long before they need to use a food bank.

Ending the need for food banks

It’s not right that anyone needs a food bank. We shouldn’t need to distribute emergency food parcels. We can change this by ensuring everyone has enough income for the essentials.

Changing policy

Our policy work explores the factors that make it more likely people will need a food bank and the policies and practices needed to ensure we all have enough money to live on.

Our work across the UK

Our work in Northern Ireland

We’re working to end the need for emergency food provision in Northern Ireland – find out what we’re doing and how you can get involved.

Our work in Scotland

In Scotland, we’re developing a new strategy that includes offering strategic grants to food banks to support their communities and identifying millions in income for people facing financial hardship.

Our work in Wales

There are 38 food banks in the Trussell community in Wales. We want to see the Welsh Government, and all political parties, work to reduce hunger and hardship in Wales.
