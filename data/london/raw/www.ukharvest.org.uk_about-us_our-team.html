Yvonne Thomson, CEO, UKHarvest

Yvonne Thomson is a highly experienced social, economic and physical regeneration specialist, qualified in community education and gaining a post graduate in Housing studies form Stirling University and Master of Philosophy in Urban Policy. She has worked tirelessly on behalf of those who are socially excluded and have no voice, opportunity or choice in life. She has worked in the public, voluntary and private sectors.

Yvonne believes that only through joint working and alliance that we will solve food poverty in the U.K. She sees an intrinsic link between homelessness and food poverty and seeks to provide a solution. Yvonne sees education as the answer to tackling food waste and UKHarvest will work with consumers and everyone right along the supply chain to tackle food waste. The harvest model of food rescue will help provide the solution as part of a four-pillar approach of rescue; education; engagement and innovation. Yvonne began founding UKHarvest in mid 2016.

Lauren Stoner, Interim COO

Lauren Stoner is an experienced charity leader, with over a decade of experience leading organisations through strategic change. She is passionate about working with organisations who empower people who may be marginalised and excluded from society to make choices and have control in their lives. 
Lauren is a passionate believer in the power of co-production and the need for organisations to be led by the voices of the people they support, in strategy, delivery and policy. She is excited to support UKHarvest through this next stage of its journey as it plans for a bright future.

Simon Thresher, Head of Operations, Logistics and Compliance

Simon has over 20 years operational management experience within the catering and hospitality industry, working with multinational corporations. His knowledge of catering from the inside, and wealth of operational experience, has allowed Simon to develop and enhance the impact of UKHarvest. Dedicated to delivery Simon has extended the boundaries of the logistical reach to encompass 7 counties along the south coast, delivered food education in person and online reaching end users globally and ensured that all food safety and health and safety requirements are met.

James Swann, Head of Programmes and Innovation

James, as head of programmes, has been instrumental in creating, developing and executing some of UKHarvest's most successful programmes and operations, including Grub Club and DARE. Through strategic planning and careful consultation of the existing food systems, James has ensured that UKHarvest are ahead of the game, rescuing more food from waste and ensuring that it reaches more households in need, year on year.

Jessica Knight, Head of Finance

Jessica has worked at UKHarvest for the last 3 years as our Head of Finance. With a strong background in accountancy, Jess is fantastically placed to make sure that all of our accounting and finance are in order, across our West Sussex HQ and the Nourish Hub in West London. 

simon brent, Awareness, Development and Education Officer

Simon began his career working in the youth sector as a play and youth worker, where he supported children and young people from marginalized and disadvantaged backgrounds—including those affected by poverty, learning difficulties, and complex family situations. With over 20 years of experience in the education sector, he transitioned into the charity sector to leverage his skills as an educator and his deep understanding of social challenges to drive meaningful and lasting impact in the community.

Liz Rhodes, Community Outreach and Engagement Manager

Liz brings diverse experience from her work in mental health and wellbeing, social work, and education services across both local authority and NGO roles. Liz has a strong person-centred approach to supporting people facing social and emotional challenges at all stages of life.

Passionate about wellbeing and connecting people with the land around us, she understands how food offers natural ways to help build community, resilience and connection. 

sian georgeou, education facilitator

Sian has been working with UKHarvest for the last 3 years, creating and delivering a range of educational programme from our NourishEd operation. Sian has a passion for reducing food waste, and supporting communities and can often be found at our community events where she shares her knowledge of eating well, and making the most of every day food shopping.

Joanna Lodge, Communications and Fundraising Manager

With a background in publishing, Jo has taken her editorial and marketing skills to create a successful career in public relations and communications for not-for-profit organisations. Jo oversees all comms for UKHarvest, covering social media, website, email, newsletter and press content.

Will Wallace, Policy and Advocacy Manager

Will joined our team in 2023 and has since played a key role in strengthening how we understand and measure our social impact. By introducing innovative systems for monitoring and evaluation, he has enabled us to gain clearer insights into the effectiveness of our mission and identify opportunities for further growth.

Will brings a strong academic background, holding a postgraduate degree in Public Policy from the University of Edinburgh, where he developed his ongoing interest in health equity, social justice and community engagement.

Sophie Cardinal, Volunteer Coordinator

Sophie first became passionate about sustainability and environmental issues during her BSc Psychology studies at the University of Surrey, where she became fascinated by the human-environment relationship and the psychology of pro-environmental behaviour.

After graduating from Bournemouth University with a MSc in Biodiversity Conservation, Sophie developed her administrative and environmental policy skills during her first role at a local authority in the minerals and waste department. 

Through experience and education, Sophie bring a wealth of passion and orchestration to UKHarvest.


Helen Conway, Finance Officer

Helen has most recently worked in Schools in Finance and previously in the classroom, she also has varied experience running her own business and last worked in food as a Buyer. In her spare time she enjoys cooking, travelling, various crafts and spending time with family and friends. She was first drawn to UKHarvest during the pandemic and how much they helped the local schools and wider community.

Logistics

Our logistics staff are integral to our operation. This group of hard-working indivduals work in our warehouse and on the roads, driving our vans to ensure the UK's surplus food is delivered to those who need it most.

The Nourish Hub

Opened in Autumn 2021 and located in London, the Nourish Hub is a space for the whole community to come together, eat well, and learn essential skills for life. Lunch is served daily by a team of decidated chefs and served to those who dine-in and to their meals-on-wheels audience. The entire hub is run by devoted staff members and volunteers.
