What we do

Youth Work

At Eat Club, we believe that cooking is more than just preparing food—it’s a gateway for London’s youth to better health, life skills, community building, and personal growth.
Learn more

Supper Clubs

Our Cook Social Supper Club combines a supper club and cooking class in London. Led by our chefs, it’s a great way to meet new people, learn culinary skills, and support a London youth food charity.

Learn more

Team Building 

Our group cooking activities combine the shared fun of cooking with the impact of supporting a youth food charity. Whether it’s team building or a celebration, we’ll tailor the experience to you.

We teach cooking skills and food budgeting to help families and young people manage resources and build financial resilience.
We combat food insecurity by teaching budget-friendly cooking, offering hot meals, and providing nutrition education.
We promote healthier choices by combining practical cooking skills with education on nutrition and well-being.
We provide inclusive food education and accredited hospitality training, opening job pathways for diverse youth, including those with learning disabilities and NEET individuals.
