Our Team

Incredible Edible Lambeth (IEL) is run by an amazing group of dedicated individuals and we are always looking for passionate change-makers to join our team.  Our team is comprised of a volunteer Board of Directors responsible for IEL’s strategic and operational decisions and for ensuring that IEL meets its statutory obligations; IEL’s paid Team Members who ensure our day-to-day running and the execution of our projects, and various volunteers who give generously of their time.

Board of Directors
