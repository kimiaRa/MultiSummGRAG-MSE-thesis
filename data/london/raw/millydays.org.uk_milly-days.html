Welcome to Milly Days

For over ten years, Milly Days has been hosting garden parties for invited charities in the beautiful grounds of a private home on the banks of the River Thames.

Our Purpose                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      At the heart of every Milly Day, from the moment guests arrive, our goal is to create a space that feels welcoming, uplifting and completely their own.

Life can be demanding, and our guests are all navigating significant challenges, including illness, recovery, caring responsibilities and other personal circumstances.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    A Milly Day offers time out. Time to breathe. Time to reconnect with joy.

A Day Designed for Our Guests                                                                                                                                                                                                                              A  day is carefully shaped around the group we are welcoming.  Whether that means boat rides along the river, swimming, petting zoos, entertainers, or simply a peaceful afternoon in the garden, the focus is always on creating moments that matter.                                        Guests can chat, laugh, relax, or explore whatever feels right for them.

Throughout the day, we serve coffee, tea and hot chocolate, alongside snacks, lunch, strawberries and a visit from the ice cream van. And of course, no Milly Day is complete without a party bag to take home.

The Difference One day can make                                                                                                                                                                                                                    Our dedicated team is at the heart of every event. They listen, they care, and they take pride in creating days filled with warmth and genuine connection. We hope every guest leaves with lasting memories moments of happiness they can carry with them long after the day ends.

There is nothing more rewarding than seeing the smiles, the laughter and the shared joy that unfold at each Milly Day.                      It is a privilege to create these special experiences and to witness the difference a single day can make.

Where your support goes

Every contribution helps us create magical Milly Days for our guests.

Your generosity ensures that each day is filled with fun, laughter, and unforgettable memories.

Your support goes towards

  • Travel to and from the venue
  • Catering
  • River boat trips
  • Petting Zoo
  • Use of heated pool and slide
  • Recreational activities and entertainment
  • A visit from the ice cream van

Costs per day of 50 people:

  • Catering                         £   1000
  • Travel                               £      650
  • River Trips                     £      300
  • Ice cream van               £      350
  • Art and craft                 £      150
  • Petting Zoo                   £       450
  • Party Bags                     £       750
