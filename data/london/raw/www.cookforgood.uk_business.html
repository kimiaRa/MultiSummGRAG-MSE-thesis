Cook up impact for your team and our community

Our community programme is transforming everyday lives and long-term outcomes for Priory Green Estate residents. And it’s mostly funded by organisations who choose to spend their team building, ESG and client entertainment budgets on a team cooking challenge.

Here's how you can get involved.

Book a team cooking challenge

Get a group together and join us for a MasterChef-style challenge, prepping and cooking around 100 meals. We’ll crank up the pressure, but support you all the way, with funky tunes in the background and professional chefs on hand to teach you new skills and keep you on track.

And once the last dish is out of the oven, you’ll get to relax with a glass of something chilled, eat the food you’ve prepared, and marvel at what you can get done when you work as a team. Doing good has never been so much fun.

Explore our teambuilding activities

Business Gives Back

Around 350,000 London families are facing food insecurity, having to cut back on essential foods, or skip meals completely. We’re asking businesses to take action this summer, by creating 5,000 family meals to share with communities. Why not use your summer social or volunteer day to give something back?

To take part, get a team together and book a Family Meal Box Challenge between 1 June and 31 August.

Go deeper with us

Get really stuck in with the work we’re doing, and deliver even greater impact for your teams and our community. That could mean booking multiple events, involving different teams, cohorts or clients, or integrating our events into your strategy days or L&D programmes. It could even mean funding a cooking course or employment programme.

We’d love to work with you on a deeper level, in a way that matches your goals, and ours. Let’s talk.
