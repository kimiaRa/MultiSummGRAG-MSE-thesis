The Felix Project and FareShare have merged to become the UK's largest food redistribution charity. By coming together we're forming a bigger and more ambitious organisation with a vision of a UK where good food is never wasted, and nobody goes hungry. Learn more about the merger here, or find out how you can get involved below.
The Felix Project and FareShare rescue high quality, surplus food that would otherwise go to waste. We redistribute food to over 8,000 community organisations across the UK supporting 1.5 million people every year.
Join us in the fight against hunger this summer. We're asking eateries across London to join our Restaurants Feed London campaign launching on World Hunger Day (28th May).
By adding an optional £1 to bills, last year's restaurants raised the equivalent of 184,000 meals, and this year we want to go even bigger. Now, we just need you!
