Eat Infinity’s shorts!
Our main supplier Infinity Foods — a workers’ co-op based in Brighton –has started a channel offering tasty recipes for quick consumption via the screen of your fondleslab. The majority of the ingredients you can get from us. Four dishes so far, all wholefood plant-based and delicious!
