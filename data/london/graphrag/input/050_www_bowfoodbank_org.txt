We are always happy to receive food donations. If you would prefer to give food for us to distribute, please take a look at our foodbank favourites list.
Our two foodbanks (at Bow and Bethnal Green) operate an open-door policy offering emergency help especially for people who may not meet the criteria for other foodbanks.
There are many opportunities for volunteering in the foodbank and behind the scenes. If you have a few hours to spare every week (or even every other week!) we would very much welcome your help.
Staff, customers, colleagues, friends and family can all play their part. With endless opportunities from simply organising a collection to completing a sponsored challenge.
