Full text:
Founded in 1986, the Soup Kitchen plays a vital role in supporting the homeless, elderly, isolated and vulnerable people of London. Our mission involves offering nourishing meals, clothing, unparalleled mental health support and fostering a strong sense of community for nearly 200 guests daily.
Through the dedicated efforts of our staff, donors and thousands of volunteers annually, our aim is to create a welcoming and safe environment where everyone who enters feels a sense of belonging.
