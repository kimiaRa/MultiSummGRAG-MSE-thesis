We are a new network of seed libraries across South London, a community of people growing plants of all kinds for health, wellbeing and biodiversity benefits. Really excited to have info from the @seedsavingnetwork in our libraries too, teaching people how to save and share seeds. Get in touch if you’d like to host a seed library, donate seeds, or simply find out more! And please share this so we can grow the network
