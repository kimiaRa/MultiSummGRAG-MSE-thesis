What is a Community Fridge?
Anyone can visit — no referral or paperwork needed. Just bring your own bag!
We are a community where members make friends, share tips, and connect with local support.
"It's become a hub of help, food, a shoulder to cry on, someone to say hi, someone to make you feel better. And it works both ways."
Merton’s Community Fridge is a long-term initiative to tackle food poverty and reduce food waste in the borough.
Every week, our volunteers and cargo bike riders collect surplus food from local shops, and businesses, then redistribute it to anyone who needs it.
By sharing good food that would otherwise go to waste, we:
-
Reduce food insecurity and hunger
-
Support physical and mental wellbeing
-
Create a safe, welcoming space for all
-
Prevent thousands of kilograms of CO₂ emissions each year
