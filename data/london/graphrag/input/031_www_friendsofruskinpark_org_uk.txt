Full text:
Ruskin Park Community Garden is a space for the local community to grow fruit and veg. Anyone is welcome to our weekly gardening sessions on Saturdays from 12pm to 3pm, where volunteers can learn how to grow veg by doing.
To find out more email communitygarden@friendsofruskinpark.org.uk
