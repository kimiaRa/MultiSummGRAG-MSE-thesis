Help your West Hampstead & Kilburn community
The West Hampstead Community Food Hub started at St Cuthbert’s Church at the beginning of the COVID pandemic, to support local families in need. That need has sadly not gone away. The Food Hub is now run at Sidings Community Centre and a team of volunteers who currently help over 140 households each week with food parcels. Here’s how you can help…
Donate Food
We rely on donations of food and funds to provide a weekly food parcel to over 140 households in the area. You can donate non-perishable food items at one of these drop-off points in the area.
Donate Funds
We’re approaching our one year anniversary - a date we didn’t think we’d need to see. With ongoing lockdown, winter fuel bills and more, our guest numbers are growing each week. If you’d like to make a donation to buy food (to supplement the items we receive at our local drop-off spots) click below.
Tell Your Friends
Sadly we don’t think that the need for this service will be going away anytime soon. To ensure we’re sustainable we would like to involve as many of the community as possible. Please follow us on Twitter & Instagram and tell your friends.
