Is unique,
both inside and outside

The restaurant enjoys an iconic location in Passatge de la Pau in Ciutat Vella, Barcelona’s old town. It fuses elements of the street’s identity with elements that  reflect its social value.

The cacti: fragility. Like many young people, cacti may look as if they are hostile, but on the inside they are moist, tender and sweet like a fruit.

The bar: dreams. At the bar, you will find words that express the anxieties, wishes and dreams of the young people who have passed through El Llindar.

The bathroom lights: personal growth. Five hundred columns of lights, with three progressive levels of illumination, represent the transition made by the young people.

The pictures: the path. The paintings by the Majorcan artist Pilar Correa have been inspired by the young people at El Llindar and the different training routes they have followed there.

The wave of lamps. The lamps that illuminate the room reflect the dynamic nature of a project in constant motion. The young people who have gained experience at La Pau make way for new colleagues, as they go on to pursue their own path, having written their name on a lamp.

The columns: emancipation, the infinite. The pillars of the restaurant go beyond the ceiling and represent all the future projects under construction for all the young people.
