How a glass bottle reuse system works

Rezero visits the first ‘pool’ of reusable wine bottles located in Germany which, after only two years of operation, already recovers 40% of the bottles sold. The initiative brings significant economic benefits for the participating wineries, as well as reducing the environmental impact of their businesses.

The right to buy without packaging clashes with reality

Rezero finds that supermarkets do not make it easy to shop with reusable packaging, in breach of regulations.

Consumers have the right to shop without generating single-use packaging, but there are barriers to enforcing this right.

RezeroLab

Towards Waste Reduction in the Nautical Sector in the Balearic Islands

A survey by Rezero detects that half of the Balearic maritime companies take waste prevention into account in their economic activity, and identifies the main obstacles to accelerating the transition towards zero waste in the sector.

Rezero, together with the MiZA, organized the conference «Marine: Zero Waste Business», a space for meeting, training and debate on waste prevention in the nautical sector of the Balearic Islands.

Zero Waste Society

How do you envision future society?
We explain the projects which give meaning to the Zero Waste future.
Together with the collaboration of different agents we build a society that values the resources provided by natural systems and integrates all materials in cyclical processes, without toxic materials, or products that are left unused.

Re-solutions and services

What can you do?
At Rezero we look for new solutions to extend the life of products and materials, to facilitate their reuse and to eliminate domestic waste generation.
We test and promote them at social, administrations and company level. Within our own fields of experience, we put knowledge and experience at the service of companies and administrations to develop proposals adapted to each need.
