Collections of food and basic products at fourteen libraries for those affected by the floods

Fourteen municipal libraries around the city’s ten districts are working in coordination with the Food Bank to offer collection points for basic foods and essential products, to send to the areas affected by the flooding in Valencia. Donations can be made from Monday, 4 November, to Friday, 8 November, during the libraries’ usual opening hours.

03/11/2024 13:49 h

The priority products being collected at these points must be packaged and are:

  • Milk
    • Conserves and pre-cooked foods (pulses, meat, fish, etc.)
    • Children’s food (porridge, powdered milk, jars of purée, etc.)
    • Nappies for babies
    • Women’s hygiene products

The fourteen libraries with collection points are:

District of Ciutat Vella

  • Biblioteca Barceloneta – La Fraternitat
    Carrer de Felícia Fuster, 8

District of L’Eixample

  • Biblioteca Fort Pienc – Ana María Moix
    Plaça del Fort Pienc, 4

District of Sants-Montjuïc

  • Biblioteca Vapor Vell
    Passatge del Vapor Vell, 1

District of Les Corts

  • Biblioteca Montserrat Abelló
    Carrer dels Comtes de Bell-lloc, 192
  • Biblioteca Les Corts – Miquel Llongueras
    Travessera de les Corts, 58

District of Sarrià – Sant Gervasi

  • Biblioteca Sant Gervasi – Joan Maragall
    Carrer de Sant Gervasi de Cassoles, 85

District of Gràcia

  • Biblioteca Jaume Fuster
    Plaça de Lesseps, 20
  • Biblioteca Vallcarca i els Penitents – M. Antonieta Cot
    Passeig de la Vall d’Hebron, 65

District of Horta-Guinardó

  • Biblioteca El Carmel – Juan Marsé
    Carrer de la Murtra, 135
  • Biblioteca Horta-Can Mariner
    Carrer del Vent, 1

District of Nou Barris

  • Biblioteca Nou Barris – Aurora Díaz Plaja
    Plaça Major de Nou Barris, 2

District of Sant Andreu

  • Biblioteca Bon Pastor – Josefina Castellví
    Carrer d’Estadella, 64
  • Biblioteca Trinitat Vella – José Barbero
    Carrer de Galícia, 16

District of Sant Martí

  • Biblioteca Camp de l’Arpa – Caterina Albert
    Carrer de la Indústria, 295

The Food Bank (Banc dels Aliments), in coordination with the Food Bank in Valencia, will be managing the shipments to areas affected by the flooding, according to the priorities at any given moment and the evolution of the emergency.

Besides the donation points, the most effective donation continues to be monetary, by bank transfer to the Banc dels Aliments de València (ES86 2100 2806 6402 0009 8998) or by Bizum to the number 38400 (subject: donation).

Mercabarna is also joining the initiatives by Barcelona City Council to provide support for those affected, offering economic aid to the Food Bank in Valencia. Companies are also organising shipments of fresh food where needs are identified.

Cookies settings

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a file downloaded to a computer for the purposes of storing and recovering information on some aspects of the user's browsing and habits.

 

Why do we use cookies?

The website uses its own and third-party cookies to facilitate and personalise your browsing, offer a better experience and measure and extract usage data that enables service to be improved.

 

Consent to the use of cookies

The City Council will only use cookies provided you consent, except in cases where they are necessary for browsing the website. You may amend your consent at any time by accessing your cookie settings.

 

What type of cookies do we use?

You can check cookie types by clicking on “more information”.

 

Changes to our Cookies Policy

The City Council may amend this policy when the current legislation at any time so requires or when there is a variation in the types of cookies used on the website. We therefore advise you to review this policy every time you access our website so you can stay properly informed of how and why we use cookies.

 

Can I remove the cookies?

Yes. Not only remove them but also block them, in general or specifically for a particular website. To remove cookies from a website you will need to go to your browser's settings (as noted in the section dealing with managing your cookie preferences) and there you will be able to find the cookies associated with the domain in question and remove them.

 

What happens if I block cookies?

You can still browse Barcelona City Council’s website, with full guarantees, without non-essential cookies. Deactivating them, however, may have effects such as the following:

  • You will not be able to share the website's contents on any social media.
  • We will not be able to adapt content to your personal preferences, such as the language it is displayed in.
  • You will not be able to geolocate through our facilities and the services we offer.
  • We will not be able to improve our website in line with the information on your browsing experience.

How to manage your cookie preferences

Most browsers automatically accept the use of cookies, but you can set your browser to reject them or warn you when a server intends to save a cookie on your device. You can do this by consulting:
