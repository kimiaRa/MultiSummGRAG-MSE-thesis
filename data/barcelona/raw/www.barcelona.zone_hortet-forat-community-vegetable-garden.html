Hortet del Forat - Community Vegetable Garden


 ❤️ Trending!   Barcelona

Discover the charm of L'Hortet del Forat in Barcelona

What is L'Hortet del Forat?

L'Hortet del Forat is a community garden of a neighborhood initiative. It is quite a victory as a result of citizen mobilizations and a meeting point for the neighborhood. Popularly known as El forat de la vergonya ('the hole of shame') is a self-built park that, over time, has remained a public space won by the neighborhood. In this environment, L'Hortet del Forat, self-managed by the community, is a resource for cultivation and ecological experimentation to strengthen social ties and improve the neighborhood's environment.

Where is L'Hortet del Forat located?

L'Hortet del Forat is located in the Sant Pere neighborhood of Barcelona. It is located in the place known as El Forat de la Vergonya, in the extreme northwest of Barceloneta.

How to get to L'Hortet del Forat?

To get to L'Hortet del Forat you can use the following means of transport:

  • Metro: lines L4 (Barceloneta stop) and L3 (Drassanes stop).
  • Bus: lines 19, 36, 45, 57, 59, 64, 91, 120, 121, 157, D20 and V17.
  • Tram: lines T4 and T5.
  • Bike: there is a Bicing station near L'Hortet del Forat.

What can you do in L'Hortet del Forat?

In L'Hortet del Forat there are many activities that can be done. Visitors can enjoy a walk through the gardens, visit the community garden and learn about organic farming. There is also a wide variety of activities for children, such as gardening workshops, cooking workshops, art workshops and leisure activities. In addition, there are a large number of cultural activities such as concerts, talks, exhibitions and festivals.

How to participate in L'Hortet del Forat?

All those interested in participating in L'Hortet del Forat can join the Local Guide Program. This initiative offers participants the opportunity to contribute their time and knowledge to improve the space. Participants will have the opportunity to participate in the organization of activities, workshops, talks, exhibitions and festivals. In addition, the participants will be able to contribute with their knowledge in the care of the gardens and the community orchard.

What can be found in L'Hortet del Forat?

In L'Hortet del Forat you can find many interesting things. There is a wide variety of plants and flowers, as well as a community garden where vegetables, fruits and herbs are grown. In addition, there is a children's play area, a rest area with hammocks and a picnic area. There is also a library with books on organic farming, a shop selling local products and a cafeteria.

What else is there to do near L'Hortet del Forat?

In addition to visiting L'Hortet del Forat, visitors can also enjoy the surrounding area. The Sant Pere neighborhood is one of the oldest neighborhoods in Barcelona and offers many interesting things to see and do. Visitors can visit the Mercat de Sant Pere, the Museu de la Xocolata and the Museu Marítim, among others. There are also many restaurants and bars in the neighborhood where you can enjoy the delicious dishes of Catalan cuisine.

Conclusions

L'Hortet del Forat is a green space self-managed by the community, which offers visitors the opportunity to enjoy a unique experience. It is located in the Sant Pere neighborhood, in Barcelona, ​​and is easily accessible by public transport. Visitors can enjoy a walk through the gardens, visit the community garden, learn about organic farming and participate in a wide variety of cultural activities. In addition, visitors can enjoy the surrounding area, visiting the neighborhood's markets, museums and bars.
