The project

A passage
of futures
Catering services
with social impact in Ciutat Vella

The restaurant La Pau is a social initiative for generating employment and gives continuity to the El Llindar School of New Opportunities.

Created in 2004, El Llindar is a dynamic centre which, every year, assists some 450 adolescents and young people who have been excluded from the educational and social system. It offers an educational programme that combines training and work, with the aim of guiding, accompanying and supporting these young people during their transition into adult life.

The development of the insertion pathways for the young people of El Llindar Fem Feina is supported by the Government of Catalonia and co-funded by the European Union (ESF+ Catalonia 2021-2027).

Situated in Passatge de La Pau, this restaurant seeks to provide a stepping stone into the future for young people who are finishing their training in catering begun at our  restaurant school, El Repartidor, where they specialize in work related with the kitchen or the dining room. At La Pau, they gain work experience and go on to become qualified workers in the field of catering.

La Pau is a reality thanks to the alliance of Enjoy BCN, a catalan family company that works in the tourism and events sector. Their collaboration is a big step for the social and labor insertion of young people.

This new undertaking introduced by El Llindar eases the young people’s integration into society and the workplace. We continue working to ensure that they can enter the job market with the skills, competencies and knowledge they need to respond to changing employment demands.

Training in a genuine working environment facilitates the transition to professional life, creating an educational, social and community-focused impact driven by an economic and social development model.
