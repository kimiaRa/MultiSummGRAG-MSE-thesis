Brighton Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) is supporting Food4Lyfe, our current new entrant farmers, who are using the opportunity to train and develop a sustainable locally grown, organic fruit and veg share, harvested on our site in Stanmer Park.
Quality local produce – which you can track from seed to table!
To sign up for a share of the crop with Food4Lyfe CLICK HERE
- Organic – We are accredited by the Soil Association, which means our produce is grown without the use of pesticides, herbicides, insecticides , GMO’s and synthetic fertilisers.
- Sustainable We endeavour to support the soil, promote biodiversity and retain soil fertility through sensitive regenerative agriculture.
- Seasonal Members will receive their share of the harvest eating seasonal, local uk produce.
- Super fresh Typically harvested in the morning and delivered to your door on the same day.
- Nutritious Studies have shown fresh organic vegetables to be higher in nutritional content and lower in toxic metals and pesticides than conventionally grown vegetables*.
- Local Reduce your carbon footprint. Help us to help you contribute to a sustainable future by keeping the production local and the food miles, associated with your food, to the absolute minimum. To support this we only deliver to Brighton and Hove addresses.
- Support A local not-for-profit community farm and the continued development of the emerging CSA national sustainable food network: www.communitysupportedagriculture.org.uk.
- Participate You and your family can come and join our growing community every Friday, get involved with seasonal Sunday gatherings, enjoy shared lunches and the opportunity to reconnect with nature.
- News Seasonal email full of on-site news including crop varieties, recipe suggestions and storage tips.
You can expect to receive a share of an interesting variety of seasonal produce. Contents and amounts will vary with the season. Whilst the ethos of the CSA is to share the risk and the reward, experience has shown that we can assure you will receive value for money throughout the scheme and when gluts happen you too will enjoy the plenty.
- A plentiful supply of leafy greens such as kale, spinach, chard, calabrese or perhaps a stir fry mix
- (In summer) rhubarb, raspberries, red and black currants, gooseberry, plums, tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers and aubergines, broad beans, runner beans, french beans, courgettes, mange tout, fennel bulbs, beetroot, new potatoes and more…
- (In winter) apples, pears, leeks, parsnips, beetroot, savoy cabbage, sprouting broccoli, turnips, pumpkins, potatoes, carrots onions, celeriac and more…
