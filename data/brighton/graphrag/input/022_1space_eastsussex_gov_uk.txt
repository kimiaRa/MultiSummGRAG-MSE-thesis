Food Banks, Community Food Hubs, and Soup Kitchens
About this category
Food banks, shelters and drop in centres that provide food or refreshments. Community food hubs include community fridges and larders and are places where people can share food including surplus from supermarkets.
Food banks and community food hubs may also supply:
- Toiletries – deodorant, toilet paper, shower gel, shaving gel, shampoo, soap, toothbrushes, tooth paste, hand wipes
- Household items – laundry liquid detergent, laundry powder, washing up liquid
- Feminine products – sanitary towels and tampons
- Baby supplies – nappies, baby wipes and baby food.
