Fighting Food Poverty In Brighton & Hove
Our Table Cards
Our flagship fundraising initiative offers diners the opportunity to add a £1 donation to their bill in support of our emergency food providers. It’s completely free for restaurants to participate, and each small contribution can help make a big difference in the lives of those who are struggling to put food on their table.
A discreet table card is placed on each table or in the bill folder explaining the voluntary £1 donation to the customer. Donations are recorded through a Love Food Share Food button which is programmed into the participating restaurant’s till.
Participating restaurants will be promoted across our website and social media platforms as official fundraising partners of Love Food Share Food. This initiative has already been very successful, and we are excited to add more local restaurants to our growing list of partners.
How It Works
Place Cards on Tables
We supply branded table cards for each table or bill folder.
Voluntary £1 Donation
You add a voluntary £1 donation to each bill using your till.
Weekly Donation Transfer
You send the donations weekly, or as agreed.
About us
Love Food Share Food is a Community Interest Company dedicated to supporting local emergency food providers. By working with community minded businesses within the food service industry, we aim to provide our beneficiaries with the financial support they need to continue their vital work. We believe that every person deserves access to nutritious food, and we're committed to making that a reality in our community.
Love Food Share Food’s Host Partners
Our heartfelt thanks go to the incredible restaurants, pubs, and cafes that have joined us in the fight against food poverty. These local heroes play a vital role in supporting emergency food providers and tackling hunger and food insecurity in our community. We’re proud to celebrate our host partners and their generosity.
