Quality Food, Affordable Prices
For a small payment each week, members of the scheme receive a great-value, subsidised shop, including a wide variety of produce. Our bags vary from week to week depending on what food is available. A typical bag includes: a range of fruit and vegetables, tinned goods, bread, pasta, oats, cereals and meats or a vegetarian alternative.
WE ARE
VOLUNTEER RUN
We are a volunteer group of local residents who are working towards making food accessible and affordable for our local community. Our services are not means tested. We are supported by the local council and through partnerships with non-profit organisations within Brighton and Hove.
