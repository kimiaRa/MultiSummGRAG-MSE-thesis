Food banks, community food hubs and low cost food
Find food banks and community food hubs in Brighton & Hove, advice about how to get food in an emergency and where you can get free and low cost food.
Food banks and community food hubs
You can find an up-to-date list of food banks and community food hubs on the Brighton & Hove Food Partnership’s (BHFP) website.
You can also get advice if you're struggling to afford food.
Food advice in an emergency
If you need immediate advice about how to get food, shopping and meal deliveries, send an email to food.access@impact-initiatives.org.uk or phone 01273 322 950.
