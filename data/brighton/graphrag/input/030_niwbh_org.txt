Food deliveries
The Network responded quickly from early March to help meet the needs of those individuals and families who were most vulnerable, isolated and unable to access adequate food and essential supplies. It is a story of great generosity and selfless volunteer effort and support to the Network, firstly from Sabri Ben Ameur, Rashida and the Muslim Forum and an anonymous donor who gifted money. Enabled by Laila Rashdan from the Network, who communicated with the Arabic-speaking women, Sabri and Rashida made weekly food deliveries drawing on their own resources and with some financial help from the Network. Laila, Rashida and Sabri received a Gold Award from the Mayor, Councillor Alex Phillips, in acknowledgement of their brilliant effort.
Later, the Table Tennis Club Food Bank provided parcels and Network volunteers Lizzie, Louise and Ruth continued with weekly deliveries of supplies to people’s homes. We are so grateful to them for doing so.
Our partnership then delivered to some 17 adults and 22 children each week for 25 weeks also providing ‘doorstep’ conversations, laughter and support while doing so.
The Network donated £500 to the Food Partnership to help meet the wider needs in the city, joined in the weekly Emergency Food Network meetings and linked to the Council’s Community Hub when referrals for more specialist support were required.
