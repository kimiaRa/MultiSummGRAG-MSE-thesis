Volunteer with us
Volunteering is a rewarding experience, whether you want to learn new skills, are considering pursuing a career in horticulture or simply want to meet new people.
Volunteering is a rewarding experience, whether you want to learn new skills, are considering pursuing a career in horticulture or simply want to meet new people.
Thank you for expressing an interest to volunteer at One Garden Brighton. Our roles range from supporting our events programmes, helping our garden team & visitor welcome. Whether you have expertise to share or simply want to learn from our team, we have a variety of roles available. Volunteer at our events and gain free access, as well as valuable insight into the industry.
We welcome volunteers from many different backgrounds and have accessible opportunities for people with all different strengths and abilities. We value our community of local volunteers and strive to create an open and welcoming environment for them to flourish. If you are not sure which roles are suitable for you, please contact outreach@onegardenbrighton.com and our dedicated Outreach Coordinator will help find the role for you.
You will provide a warm welcome to the gardens and enjoy engaging with our visitors in a meaningful way, answering questions and promoting the values of One Garden Brighton.
You may also be asked to help with tours of the garden and will be happy to answer any questions from our visitors about the garden, the link with Plumpton College and our horticulture education offerings.
- Become part of the dedicated team who are passionate about One Garden Brighton
- Showcase this unique and inspiring space to our visitors
- Learn new skills and gain experience
- Full training provided
- Invovled in nature
- Meet new people
The walled garden at Stanmer Park is a site of great local and historical importance. You will be part of a dedicated team bringing to life a free to enter destination. It is a unique attraction, combining innovative and creative horticulture and enriching community spaces for local people.
Our volunteering roles are flexible, and we will work around your availability. Roughly speaking, garden & glasshouse volunteer is half a day minimum commitment and for our events volunteer is a minimum of 3 hours per day.
As part of our 'thank you' to you for volunteering your time we offer:
- 15% Discount at One Kitchen - seated dining experience
- 15% Discount at One Market - fresh local produce, gifts & plant sales
- A full induction process and tour of the themed gardens
For any questions, please email outreach@onegardenbrighton.com
