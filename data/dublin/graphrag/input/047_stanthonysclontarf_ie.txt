House Welcome
Welcome to St Anthony’s House, Clontarf
The Community Centre of St. Anthony’s Church
St. Anthony’s House is a welcoming and vibrant community hub located in the heart of Clontarf. We offer a wide range of activities, classes, and events for people of all ages and backgrounds.
Whether you’re looking to meet new people, try something new, or find a supportive space, you’ll find a warm welcome here.
Join a Class or Course
From fitness and wellness to creative arts and lifelong learning, our classes and courses offer something for everyone.
Trying a new class is a great way to meet others in the community — especially if you’re new to the area.
️ Meals on Wheels & Community Lunches
We proudly provide Meals on Wheels for those who need extra support at home.
Everyone is also welcome to join us for our in-house lunch, served daily at 12 noon – a chance to relax, enjoy a meal, and connect with others.
️ Facilities & Rentals
Planning an event or activity? We offer a range of beautiful and versatile spaces:
- Rooms and halls available for hire
- Our beautifully renovated old church is available to book
- Minibus available for community groups and outings
Thank You to Our Supporters
We are sincerely grateful to FÁS and the Health Board Executive for their continued support of our mission and community work over the years.
New Members Are Always Welcome!
Whether you’re just visiting or looking to get involved long-term, we’d love to meet you.
