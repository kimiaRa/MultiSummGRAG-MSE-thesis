Mary Aikenhead Day Centre
Through the Mary Aikenhead Day Centre we minister to older people. This Centre, in Donnybrook, Dublin, works in co-operation with statutory and other voluntary agencies and includes lay colleagues working alongside Sisters of Charity and many volunteers, is open five days a week. It aims to foster the well-being of older persons in body, mind, emotions and spirit and to enhance the quality of their lives; to provide a service that fosters the core values of dignity, compassion, justice and respect; and to support the family and carers of the older person.
Facilities include lunch, keep-fit classes, hairdressing, and physiotherapy. Outings are organised to places of interest.
