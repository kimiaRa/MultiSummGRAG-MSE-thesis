- Library Garden
From a tiny seed great things can grow …
The Library Garden on Tallaght campus has grown and evolved over the years but it has always been a peaceful place to sit and take a few minutes out of what can be a busy university schedule. In the garden you will be surrounded by plants of different textures and colours, and the sound of the gentle trickle of water from the fountain. Staff and students are free to step out for a few minutes of fresh air, or sit down for lunch any time of the day.
If you want to be involved with the garden, keep up to date with developments, or just love gardening you can join the Viva Engage group Gardening Tribe TU Dublin.
Library Elemental Living Lab
Around the garden you’ll also notice a number of tubs, tubes, and barrels. This is part of an exciting collaboration with the School of Engineering, Built Environment & Apprenticeships - The Library Elemental Living Lab. Officially launched in September 2025, you can read more about the Living Lab here.
