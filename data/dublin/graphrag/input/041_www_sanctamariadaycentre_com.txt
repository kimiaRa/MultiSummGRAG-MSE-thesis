About Us
The Sancta Maria Day Care Centre is open 5 days a week. We offer a safe, homely place for elderly residents of our community to socialise. We provide lunch and afternoon tea to clients. We also offer a meals on wheels service which delivers a hot, freshly prepared, nutritional meal to your door.
We collect our clients each day from home and drop them back every evening. They get a four course meal every day at lunchtime. In addition we offer activities which include crafts, bingo, chair exercise, music, TV room etc.
There are additional services such as facilities of a wet room, hairdressing and chiropody.
We also have a Meals on Wheels service in operation. Meals are freshly prepared each day, ensuring our clients get a friendly hello and a fresh nutritional meal, Monday through Friday, delivered to their door.
Our services aim to provide an independent lifestyle. Whether it’s in a our day care or through meals on wheels, we aim for safe, social interaction, nutritional meals and peace of mind.
