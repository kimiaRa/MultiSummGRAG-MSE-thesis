Description: Ranelagh Community Response is a registered charity that works with the National Meals on Wheels Network to serve meals and provide other assistance to those in need in the communities of Ranelagh, Rathmines, Rathgar, Donnybrook, Milltown, Ballsbridge, Sandymount, and Irishtown.

Seeing the community of Ranelagh through COVID and beyond.
Through our meals delivery, we support people in the Southeast area from Ranelagh, Rathmines, Rathgar, Donnybrook, Milltown, Ballsbridge, Sandymount, and Irishtown.
High-quality, nutritional meals
We make everything in our kitchen using the freshest ingredients. We only serve food that is delicious and nutritious.
Caring in the community
When it comes to taking care of our community, we don’t stop at food. We assist with a variety of small tasks to help our community looking and feeling its best. How will you get involved?
"The Community Care Group that we have go out of their way to make sure if somebody needs a lift to the hospital or the hairdresser, or wherever, that's done."
