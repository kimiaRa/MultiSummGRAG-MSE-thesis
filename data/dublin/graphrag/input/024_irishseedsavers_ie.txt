Conservation Through Use
As guardians of Ireland’s rich food heritage, we protect and preserve rare, traditional seed varieties, heritage apples, and the invaluable cultural knowledge they carry. By embracing sustainable practices and sharing these treasures, we not only safeguard the past but also nurture a thriving future for generations to come.

What We Do at
Irish Seed Savers
From our conservation sites in County Clare, we support a growing movement of people who believe in biodiversity, community, and food sovereignty. We save seeds. We grow trees. We teach. We connect people to Ireland’s food legacy. Through our national Seed Bank, heritage orchards, education programmes, and Seed Guardian network, we keep genetic diversity alive — in gardens, fields, and farms across the country.
Conservation
Maintaining a living heritage seed bank and orchard, preserving open-pollinated seeds and fruit varieties for future generations.
Protection
Safeguarding heritage crops from environmental threats through stewardship, policy support, and strong advocacy efforts.
Utilisation
Ensuring heritage crops thrive through broad use, traditional knowledge, appropriate technology, and resilient food systems.
Education
Inspiring all ages to reconnect with nature, learn sustainable growing, conserve biodiversity, and strengthen local food systems.

Rooted in Heritage,
Growing for the Future
ISSA began with a simple but vital purpose: to protect Ireland’s rapidly diminishing food crop heritage from extinction. Founded in 1991 by Anita Hayes, the organisation emerged from a growing awareness that Ireland’s traditional, open-pollinated seed varieties and heritage fruit trees were vanishing — and with them, the genetic diversity, knowledge, flavour, and resilience they carried.
