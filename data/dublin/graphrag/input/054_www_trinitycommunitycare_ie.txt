Trinity Community Care CLG
Trinity Community Care Clg is a HSE approved Home Care Agency. Our services include 24 hour Home Care, Palliative Care, Community Care, Meals on Wheels, Day Centre Support, 24 hour Responder Service.
TRINITY COMMUNITY CARE CLG
The service provides support for a wide variety of people in the local community, including:
- Older Persons
- Persons with Dementia/Alzheimers
- Persons with Palliative Care Needs
- Persons with a Physical or Sensory Impairment
- Persons with an Intellectual Disability
- Persons who attend Mental Health Services
- Families Under Stress
- Children with Special Needs
